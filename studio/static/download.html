A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Make room for your ideas.

Read the full setup guide
01

Get the app running.

Use the source project with Python, or produce a Windows installer using the included build instructions. The installer is a separate build step.

02

Bring your local models.

Connect an installed Ollama text model and select local image, video, or Piper voice files in Settings. Large AI model weights are not part of this download.

03

Keep creating offline.

Once the app, packages, and models are on your computer, work without cloud inference. Your projects, preferences, and feedback stay on your machine.

A practical start for your PC.

For your RTX 3060 and 32 GB RAM, start with a modest quantized text model and Stable Diffusion 1.5. Model fit and speed depend on your card’s VRAM. Video generation needs more resources.

This release learns saved preferences and task routing from feedback. It does not automatically retrain the large models. Sung-song generation and neural voice cloning are not included.
